# Visual assets used in the redesign

- The company logo was supplied directly by the user and is used as supplied.
- `public/images/brand/original-cycle-hero.webp` is a new built-in ImageGen concept, generated without reference photos, with no requested third-party names, logos or watermarks. The full-resolution PNG is preserved alongside it.
- Inner-page banners use original CSS shapes and gradients instead of borrowed photographs.
- The homepage no longer displays third-party client logos, sub-brand graphics, exhibition imagery or copied slider artwork.
- The news archive uses original CSS year cards instead of generated replicas of exhibition photographs. Category covers use isolated component renders rather than cycling photos with embedded branding.
- Product illustrations retained from the previous image recreation are reference-based AI renders. Their visual origin is documented in `generated.json`; they are not independently rights-cleared assets.
- Archived original assets remain in the repository for reference. Keeping an archive does not mean it is displayed in the redesigned homepage.

These changes reduce borrowed branding in the design. They are not a certification that every retained asset is free of copyright or trademark claims.
